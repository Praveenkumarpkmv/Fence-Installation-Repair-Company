Our Story

From One Truck to 2,400+ Fences

Apex Fence Solutions started in 2001 with one pickup truck, a trailer, and two brothers who believed a fence should outlast the mortgage. What began as a two-man crew working weekends has grown into the region's most trusted fencing team — but our founding principle hasn't changed: do it right, or don't do it at all.

Every project is still overseen by a family member. Every post is still set at frost-depth with high-density concrete. And every customer still gets our personal cell number in case anything needs attention after the job.

  • 100% in-house master crews
  • Family-owned since day one
  • Lifetime gate sag-free guarantee
  • Eco-friendly sourced materials

What Guides Us

Our Core Values

These aren't posters on a wall. They're the rules we hold ourselves to on every single job.

Honest Estimates

We quote what the job actually costs — no bait-and-switch pricing, no hidden disposal fees, no surprise material surcharges.

Master Craftsmanship

Every post set at frost-depth. Every panel custom-assembled. We build fences that handle gale-force winds and seasonal ground shifts.

In-House Crews

We never subcontract. Every installer is background-checked, trained by our master craftsmen, and accountable to the family name on the truck.

Responsible Sourcing

We source sustainably harvested cedar, virgin (not recycled) vinyl, and powder-coated aluminum — materials that last decades, not seasons.

Stand-Behind Warranty

15-year craftsmanship warranty on installs, 12-month on repairs. Backed in writing, honored without argument.

Respect for Your Time

We show up when we say we will, finish when we said we'd finish, and leave your property cleaner than we found it.
