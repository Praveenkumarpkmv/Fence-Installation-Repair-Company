Common Questions

Pricing & Estimate FAQ

Quick answers to the questions we hear most about our quotes and pricing.

Is the quote really fixed-price?

Yes, 100% guaranteed. Once our technician completes the on-site laser measurement and you approve the itemized proposal, that exact price is locked for 60 full days. We absorb any hidden ground conditions or extra concrete needed—the price will never increase unless you explicitly request a scope change (such as adding extra gates or upgrading to premium post caps).

Are permits and HOA approvals included?

Yes. Standard municipal building permits, underground utility markouts (811 Call Before You Dig), and comprehensive HOA approval assistance are fully handled by our team. We generate stamped architectural review drawings, plat line markups, and material specification packets for your HOA board so you get hassle-free first-round approval.

Do you charge for the on-site measurement?

Never. Our on-site consultation, digital laser boundary measurement, grade slope assessment, and physical material sample review are 100% free with zero sales pressure and no obligation. Your detailed quote is sent within 24 hours, and you have 60 days to decide.

What's included in the per-foot price?

Our transparent linear-foot pricing includes turnkey installation: post-hole augering 36"–42" deep, 120 lbs commercial concrete per post, premium pickets/panels, hot-dipped rustproof fasteners, one standard 4ft walk gate with heavy-duty latch hardware, full site cleanup, and our written craftsmanship warranty.

Can I get a quote without an on-site visit?

We can give you an immediate ballpark estimate over the phone or via our online estimator tool using your estimated footage. However, to issue a guaranteed, fixed-price contract, an estimator visits to inspect elevation changes, ground hardness, tree roots, and utility lines so there are never surprise change orders later.

Do you offer discounts for large projects?

Yes! We offer tiered volume discounts for residential properties over 250 linear feet, as well as a "Good Neighbor" discount when you and an adjacent neighbor replace shared property boundary fences at the same time. We also offer special discounts for military veterans, first responders, and senior citizens.
