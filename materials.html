Buyer's Guide

How to Choose the Right Material

Three things to weigh before you commit — learned from 25 years of installing all four.

Total Cost of Ownership

Cedar costs less upfront but needs staining every 3-4 years. Vinyl costs more but requires zero maintenance. Over 20 years, vinyl often wins on total cost.

Match Your Home

A mid-century modern home pairs beautifully with horizontal aluminum. A cottage loves white vinyl picket. A farmhouse shines with cedar. Material should feel intentional.

Climate & Environment

Coastal salt air destroys untreated wood — go aluminum. Wet climates favor vinyl. High-wind zones need steel-reinforced posts. We'll tell you what works for your zip code.
